Writer-diverse accuracy, x/× confusion, assignment/variable AST match and rare confident-error gates are **BLOCKED_WITH_EVIDENCE**. No reported percentage is manufactured from synthetic replay.

The checked-in empty corpus hashes after validation/canonical serialization to `ee3506543d505fc0cd0ba0fdb6286c4d35f0c530a5a4cbf30bea9ba9bac1715f`. Validation returns zero counts and an explicit blocked status. Freezing refuses empty evaluation data; new lock/report paths use exclusive creation so older evidence is not overwritten. Writer IDs cannot straddle development/evaluation, sessions cannot straddle writers, document IDs cannot repeat, stroke annotations cannot overlap, and missing labels/exclusions or complete AST truth are rejected. Provenance/rights remain human attestations and need review; schema validation cannot prove who drew the strokes.

Classifier reports retain independent numerator/denominator, top-one, top-three recall, macro F1/per-class precision/recall, absent-class lists, confusion counts, operator accuracy and decimal recall. Empty denominators produce null, never 0% or 100%. Grouping uses one-to-one exact source-stroke-set matching; duplicate predictions count against precision. Expression AST equality uses independently annotated canonical operator trees. Equal answers alone do not imply an AST match. Answer/AST/coverage/abstention/wrong-accepted metrics separate automatic and user-corrected subsets. Wilson 95% binomial intervals are provided with the stated limitation that they do not model writer clustering; small writer/sample counts cannot establish the ≤1% error target.
